## Method

1. Base rate from BlackBerry's own 50 scheduled results releases since 2014 (24 Form 6-K
   2014 to 2019, 26 Form 8-K 2020 to 2026; SEC EDGAR acceptance clocks and wire times decide
   morning versus evening; Nasdaq daily bars, cross-checked against stockanalysis.com on all
   42 two-source events with zero differences; a Nasdaq feed defect on 10 unrelated sessions was
   detected and worked around): up 25/50 = 50 % overall (Wilson 90 % interval 39 to 61 %),
   12/20 = 60 % over the last 20, 7/12 over the last 12, 12/26 = 46 % over the 8-K era. The
   60 % last-20 figure is the most favourable rolling window since 2018 (range 35 to 60 %), so
   it is shrunk hard: start point 0.51 to 0.52.
2. Adjustments, each sized and independently checked: consensus revenue ($144 to 146 M) sits
   1 to 2 % above the guidance midpoint ($137 to 148 M) and EPS consensus ($0.04) at the top of
   the guide, but the vendor's own history shows the same configuration before three of the last
   four prints, three of which rose, so 0. Estimate revisions flat (0 raised, 0 lowered in four
   weeks): 0. No preliminary release or guidance change on file (both 2023 pre-announcements were
   negative and preceded -12 % and -16 % prints): +0.01. Positioning (short interest down to
   6.2 % of float, retail sentiment bullish, put/call 0.55): -0.01, cancelling. Drift (-4 % over
   21 sessions), drawdown (-38 % from the July high) and sign alternation: 0, none statistically
   meaningful in this sample. Peer read-through mixed: 0. Result p(up) about 0.52, so UP, barely.
3. Magnitude: median absolute move 8.9 % over all 50 prints, 9.3 % over the last 20, 10.0 % over
   the last 12; mean 12 % over the last 8; options-implied move 12.8 to 13.3 % (TipRanks
   2026-09-15, OptionSlam weekly to 2026-09-25), which realised moves have covered at 0.7 to 0.9x

   6.4 to 7.0 %, the one dependence in the data with p below 0.05). Point estimate 9 %. On the
   harness's definition the four morning prints since 2020 realised 0.5x the close-to-close move

## What would disprove it

A close on 2026-09-24 below the 2026-09-23 close; the call expects that about 48 % of the time.
The mechanism for a fall: revenue below the $142.5 M guidance midpoint or adjusted EBITDA below
$25 M, or fiscal 2027 guidance not raised or only its floor raised (the 2025-12-18 pattern, -13 %).
The mechanism for a rise: revenue above $148 M with the top of the fiscal 2027 range raised and
QNX backlog above $1 B. A realised move under 3 % (5 of the last 20 prints) would show the
magnitude anchor was wrong for this print. Before the event: a preliminary-results 8-K, a switch to
an evening release, or a move beyond 10 % in the 2026-09-23 close would void this call.
