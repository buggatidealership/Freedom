## What would disprove it

A close on 2026-09-23 above the 2026-09-22 close; the call itself expects that about 44 % of the
time. The mechanism that would produce it: organic net sales at or above -1 % with improving
volume and the fiscal 2027 ranges tightened upward (the 2022-09-21 +5.7 % and 2026-07-01 +8.5 %
pattern). The mechanism for a large down move: organic sales at -4 % or worse or a guidance cut
(the 2017 and 2018 September pattern). A realised move well under 3.2 % would not disprove the
direction but would show the magnitude should have leaned more on the quiet-September subsample.
