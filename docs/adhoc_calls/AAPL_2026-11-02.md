## How I think the crowd digests it (my own read; H)
- The September quarter itself is close to known: the street sits at the top of Apple's own range, and Apple usually lands at or just over the top. A revenue beat of 1-2 % and EPS around $2.00-2.05 is the modal print. In this ledger, a met table at the highs gives no up-side information.
- The line the crowd will grade is the **December-quarter guide**, above all the **gross-margin range**. Memory prices kept rising after July, Apple said they would, and the tariff refund (~1 pt) will not repeat forever. A December GM guide at or below ~46 % is the July shape again. A December revenue guide strong on iPhone 18 demand but "supply constrained" reads as a ceiling on the holiday quarter.
- The up case: the iPhone 18 cycle is strong enough that Apple guides December revenue growth into the mid-teens with GM held at ~47 %, which would mean Apple is absorbing memory through pricing and mix. Then the haven bid extends, as in Jan-26.
- Lessons that bear: a defensive or haven at the highs mostly trades the tape and the forward line (KO, PM, NOVN; "no UP calls on defensives at highs"). The forward-line gate: the implied next period against its consensus names the reaction. The threshold-habit rule does not apply, because 6 of 8 is under 7 of 8, but the habit leans the same way. Outside the AI-capex tape a beaten table is bought, but only if it is beaten above the whole range, and the range here includes the GM line that the July crowd sold.
