- Five straight prints sold, 44 % off the high: this is no longer a "beat gets sold" story, it is a growth-deceleration story the crowd has been pricing for a year. Guided growth has stepped down 17.6 → 16.2 → 13.4 → 11.7 %; a Q4 forecast under ~10 % growth with a 2027 margin or content-cost message is the sale, a Q4 forecast at ~11-12 % with the ad tier and pricing holding is the relief.
- Positioning after five sold prints and a 44 % drawdown is the UP argument: the holders who sell on prints have mostly sold. The gap decides (11 of 12), and a flat-to-positive gap into this positioning can run. That keeps P(up) near the coin, not at the regime's 0.3.
- The tape: risk-off, yields 5 %+, good prints sold across sectors this month (GE and IBKR among them). Netflix is not an AI-capex name, so the memory/semis narrative does not apply; the consumer-subscription narrative does (pricing power vs churn).
- Net read: in-line Q3, Q4 forecast at the low end of what the street carries (revenue growth ~10-11 %), 2027 framed as a margin year; gap −3 to −6 %, close near the gap. P(up) 0.42.
