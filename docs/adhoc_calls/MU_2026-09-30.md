## Method changes since the six-call review

Applied here for the first time, fixed before any evidence was gathered: (1) the start point is a
pre-registered formula, `p_up_0 = (ups + 2) / (n + 4)` over all clean quarterly prints, not a window
chosen per call; (2) a conditional may move the sign only if it has at least 15 prints and its 90 %
interval excludes 50 %, capped at 0.05; (3) narrative inference is capped at 0.05 in total and must
name the print-versus-bar mechanism; (4) a gatherer is dedicated to the bar the market carries
(guide, consensus, whisper, the guide the Street needs); (5) both skeptics must give their own
probability and a named trigger for the opposite case; (6) the record states an UP and a DOWN
mechanism so the resolution can say which fired.

## Mechanisms

UP (about 0.43; typical +8 to +13 %): the print clears the raised bar on every line and the guide
clears it by a margin, the June 2026 and December 2025 template. Triggers: Q4 revenue at or above
$53 B with gross margin at or above 87 % and EPS at or above $33; a November-quarter guide midpoint
at or above $60 B with gross margin at or above 88 % and EPS at or above $38; 2027 HBM4 supply sold
out at higher prices; FY2027 capex held near the telegraphed mid-$40 B with free cash flow rising.

DOWN (about 0.57; typical -3 to -8 %): "sell the record", a clean beat of the company's own guide
that merely meets the Street. Triggers: Q4 revenue $50 to 51.5 B with gross margin about 86 % and
EPS $31 to 32; a November-quarter guide midpoint at or below $57.5 B or gross margin at or below
87 %; FY2027 capex framed at $45 to 50 B or more; any admission that 2027 HBM pricing is still under
negotiation. That reproduces September 2025 (-2.8 %) and March 2026 (-3.8 %). The December 2024
-16 % tail is reserved for a guide that actually misses.

## What would disprove it

A close on 2026-10-01 above the 2026-09-30 close; the call itself expects that about 43 % of the
time, and expects the average outcome to be slightly positive. Before the print: a sell-off of 4 to
5 % today would wash out the tape as before the September 2024 (+14.7 %) and June 2026 (+15.7 %)
prints and would argue for dropping the lean to a pure coin flip; a live options re-read showing the
Oct 2 straddle under about 6 % or over about 9 % of spot would re-size the magnitude. At the print:
a November-quarter guide at or above $60 B with gross margin at or above 88 % and a Thursday close
above about $1,120 falsifies the lean and its mechanism; a rise on an in-line guide with capex at or
above $50 B would show the "sell the record" story is stale. A move under 3 % would say the options
overpriced the event; a move beyond the band is plausible, since half of the last eight prints
exceeded 10 %. The anchor itself, 57/43 with an interval that includes 50 %, would be retired if
future prints lift the all-sample up-share until the interval excludes 50 %.
