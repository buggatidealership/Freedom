## How the crowd digests it (my view, separate from the arithmetic)

The headline tomorrow is "BMW car margin near zero" on a stock already at 2020 lows, after Mercedes's cut a week earlier taught the sector that Q4 carries the costs. The buyers' case is that a €2 B+ charge, Gen5 retired, and management cuts are the clean-up before Neue Klasse, and the stock is 40 % off its high at ~8× a trough EPS; that case needs a number for 2027 that BMW has so far declined to give (the CMD lowered the 2028 frame). Without that number the session sells the warning the way June did, smaller because the base is lower and part of the sector repricing is done. I expect an open around −3 to −4 %, a test lower in the first hour, and a close in the −3 to −6 % range. What would make me wrong: a sector-wide bid (Mercedes/VW up ≥ 1 % at the open) plus a quantified small charge — the VOW3 template.
