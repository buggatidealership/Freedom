## How I think the crowd digests it (my own read; H unless marked)
- Coca-Cola's crowd pays for two things on print day: organic growth at or above 5 % and another nudge to the EPS guide. It has been paid three times in 2026 (+5.0, +3.9) and on the last October print (+4.1). When it is not paid, it barely sells (−0.6 median), because the defensive bid absorbs a "fine" print. That asymmetry is the whole call: the up leg is +3 to +5 and the down leg is −0.5 to −2, so even at a coin-flip probability the called-side expectation favours UP.
- The PM lesson cuts the other way: a crowded defensive at the highs can gap −6 % on a guide nuance. The difference is the guide position: PM's street sat above the company's line and the hold read as a cut; Coca-Cola's street (3.30) sits inside the +9-10 % range already raised in July, so a hold is a hold. A volume slowdown against tougher comps is the one nuance that could do a PM.
- Net read: comparable EPS ~0.90, organic growth ~5-6 %, volume flat to +1 %, FY guide held or EPS range nudged; gap +1 to +2 %, close +1.5 to +3 %. P(up) 0.50.
