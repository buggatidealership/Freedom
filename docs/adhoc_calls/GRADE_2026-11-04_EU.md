## Lessons (hypotheses with disproofs)
1. **A warning is a lowered bar, not a regime; the quarter is graded against the corridor it set (BMW).** After a profit warning that is already in the reference, the print's sign is set by the operating line against the new corridor: BMW's 3.4 % Q3 margin against a −0.5 to +0.5 % FY corridor and a 2.3 % Q2 was a beat, and the unquantified charge and negative FCF were ignored. Rule: on a post-warning print, map "quarter line above the lowered corridor" to A with P(up|A) ≥ 0.60 regardless of the charge detail; the warning-day analogs (June −7, Mercedes −3.9) apply to the warning day, not the results day. Record: 0 of 1 for the warning-cell reading. Disproof: the next post-warning results print with a beat on the operating line closes ≤ −2 %.
2. **The lowered-bar-cleared configuration is 5 of 5 as a pattern and I have called it once (AZN).** NOVN +1.0, MRK +3.9, AZN +7.0, NVO ≈ +5.5, BMW +5.5 (cyclical form). Both docs today contained the configuration in their alternative paragraph and ranked a sold-analog above it. Rule in force: when a company-lowered or street-lowered bar is in place and the print clears it on the headline line, the side is UP at the A up-leg; no met-line analog (Aug template, warning cell, buyback-size cell) outranks it. Disproof: two of the next four cleared lowered bars close ≤ −1 %.
3. **European own-cell rule is 4 of 7 and the lopsided-down clause failed its first test (NVO).** Hits ASML, HSBC, SHEL, AZN (cells 7-8 of 8 one way, prints that matched the cell's direction); misses NOVN, VOW3 (cells over a de-rated base), NVO (lopsided-down cell over a de-rated base with a raise). Amendment: a lopsided-down cell on a ≥ 25 %-de-rated European name governs only on a met or cut table; on a raised table or a cleared lowered bar the table governs (the Oct 29 US table rule, now extended to Europe). Disproof: a raised table on a de-rated European name with a lopsided-down cell closes ≤ −2 % in the next three such prints.
4. **A one-observation habit is not a habit (NVO "9M narrowing").** The Nov 2025 narrowing was n = 1 and carried P(B) 0.45. Rule (restating the Oct 29 seasonal-cell retirement): a company-behaviour cell needs n ≥ 3 before it moves a scenario weight by more than 0.10. Disproof: none needed; a process rule.
5. **Sector-day term on European prints (BMW, NVO).** DAX +2.5 to +2.8 % on the midterm result; both names ran ~3 points past it.
