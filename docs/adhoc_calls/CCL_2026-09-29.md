## What would disprove it

A close on 2026-09-29 above 22.14; the call itself expects that about 44 % of the time. The
mechanism that would produce it: the expected Q3 beat paired with full-year adjusted EPS held at
or above about $2.15 (the Q4 fuel hit absorbed by yield and cost offsets), a Q4 EPS guide at or
above about $0.26, Q4 constant-currency net yields guided at or above about +1 %, and explicit
language that cumulative advanced bookings for 2027 are ahead of last year on both price and
occupancy with no mention of close-in Caribbean discounting; off a 52-week low with short interest
up a third, that is the template for the squeeze the call-tilted smile hints at. The mechanism for
the fall: a full-year cut of $0.10 or more on fuel, a Q4 yield guide at or below about +0.5 %, or
close-in discounting acknowledged, the June 2026 and March 2026 templates that produced -4 to -5 %.
A realised move under 2.5 % would show the magnitude should have leaned on neither the options nor
the matching cells; a move beyond -9.5 % would say both understated a fuel-driven guide reset.
