## Method

1. Base rate from the company's own 40 results 8-Ks (item 2.02) accepted since 2018, joined to
   dividend-adjusted daily closes (stockanalysis.com adjusted series, cross-checked against the
   Nasdaq historical table on every print; a second, independent recomputation by a skeptic
   found zero discrepancies). 33 prints were after the close and 7 before the open, and each was
   graded on its own next-close or same-close window. Three 2018 rows are the Jefferies Group
   subsidiary's releases and two are confounded preliminary releases; excluding them changes
   nothing material. The Spectrum Brands stock dividend of 2019-09-27 falls inside one window
   and is handled by the adjusted series. Up 24/40 = 60 % (Wilson 90 % interval 47 to 72 %);
   58 % in the November-fiscal-year era (n = 31); 50 % over the last 20, 4/12 over the last 12,
   2/8 over the last 8. Start point p(up) 0.58.
2. Setup conditioning (measured, small samples, shrunk): the weak recent record is confounded
   by setup. Ten of the last twelve prints followed a positive 21-session run-up (3 of 10 up);
   prints entered on a negative 21-session drift, as today (-10.3 % from 53.13 on 08-26 to
   47.68), were up 13 of 17, and after a down print (June's was -9.15 %) 11 of 15. Spearman
   correlation between drift and r24 is -0.26, a tilt rather than a rule. Net +0.04.
3. Fiscal-Q3 seasonality (n = 7 parent prints, 2019 to 2025): up 3 of 7, median absolute move
   2.1 %, nothing beyond 6.8 %. Net -0.03 and a smaller magnitude.
4. Fundamentals and news (inference, capped at -0.10): the segment consensus asks for a record
   investment-banking quarter ($1.32 B, advisory $813 M against $674 M in May) in a summer
   window where calendar-Q3 announced M&A fell about half quarter on quarter and IPO proceeds
   outside SK hynix were small; Oppenheimer's raised estimate ($1.274 B) sits below that bar;
   the Radiant World and Point Bonita exposures (markdowns in the last three quarters) have no
   conference call to frame them; three target cuts against one raise in September; June's
   print missed by 12 % and fell 9 %. Against that: the stock is 18 % below its June level, on
   five-month lows, at about book value, with short interest up by half in a month and the EPS
   bar cut to $1.00 to $1.04, so the setup reads washed out rather than crowded. Net -0.06.
   Result p(up) about 0.53, so UP, barely.
5. Magnitude: median absolute reaction 2.9 % (all 40), 3.1 % (fiscal-year era), 3.3 % (last
   20), 2.1 % (fiscal Q3 only); mean 3.6 to 4.2 %; the last eight prints averaged 5.1 % with

## What would disprove it

A close on 2026-09-29 below the 2026-09-28 close; the call itself expects that about 47 % of the
time. The mechanism that would produce it: investment-banking net revenues at or below the May
quarter's $1.21 B, advisory well under about $750 M, a newly quantified Radiant World or Point
Bonita mark, or diluted EPS under about $0.90 with a compensation ratio at or above 54 %, the
June 2026 and March 2025 templates that produced -5 % or worse. The mechanism for a rise: a
record IB line at or above about $1.30 B with no new credit charge, which on a stock at
five-month lows and book value has produced the after-down-print relief seen in 11 of 15 cases.
A realised move well under 3.7 % would not disprove the direction but would show the magnitude
should have leaned on the fiscal-Q3 subsample.
