## How I think the crowd digests it (my own read; H unless marked)
- IBM's crowd tonight is two groups. The post-warning holders want proof that July was execution, not demand: did the delayed Z and software deals close, is software growth ex-Confluent still 4 %+, is the $1 B FCF increase still credible with H1 flat. The rotation buyers of the last week bought IBM as the place to hide from the AI-capex sale (XLK −5.5 %, IBM +4.8 %); they are not fundamental holders and leave on the first soft line.
- The data layer will probably read fine: EPS beats are habitual (4 of 4, +0 to +9 %), infrastructure will look better than seasonal because Q2's deals slipped into it, and software prints ~6 % cc with Confluent inside. The digestion layer is where IBM moves 6-8 %: the sell-side will strip the acquired revenue (organic software ~3-4 % with Transaction Processing still falling) and look at Q3 FCF against the $1 B increase. Management held that guide through a −25 % day; Q3 is the quarter it either proves it or softens it. A softened FCF line or "about 4 %" revenue reads as the second cut in three months and the stock loses the −6 to −9 % it has habitually lost on cash and growth disappointments.
- The relief case is real and large: ACN, RPM and STZ this autumn were bought from de-rated bases on a beat with a forward positive (3 of 3 in my own ledger), and IBM's Jan-25/Jan-26 prints show +5 to +13 when software accelerates with the FCF guide intact. What stops me there is the set-up: the d5 +4.8 % chase into a binary print is the shape this round's crowd has sold 3 of 4 times, and the base is de-rated, which the trust section says is where the sold-print regime applies, not where it is exempt.
- Net read: EPS ~2.95, revenue ~$16.9-17.0 B (+2-3 %), software +6 % cc (organic ~4 %), consulting flat, infrastructure −2 to −4 %, FCF ~$2.3-2.6 B, revenue guide held at 4-5 % with "toward the low end" language, FCF guide held in words with a hedge; gap −3 to −6 %, close near the gap. P(up) 0.42.
