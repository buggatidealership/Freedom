**What I missed and how to compute it next time:**
1. An after-hours print is not a measurement of the next close when a management call sits between it and the open. STZ now has two cases of an after-hours or opening drop of ~4-5 % reversing to an up close (Apr-2025, Oct-2026) against four that carried. Next time: for post-release calls on names with a pre-open call, cap P(carry) at ~0.6 and score the reversal scenario at ≥ 0.25 when (a) the stock is within 5 % of a 52-week low, (b) the release beat on EPS and revenue, and (c) the negative is a volume or margin detail management can reframe with a current-month datum.
