### Lessons applied

- JEF (measure the run-in and how often beats were sold): measured. The stock did not run in (run5
  -1.95 %, -18.8 % from the June high, lagged the TAIEX all September). The draft read this as room
  to re-rate; the skeptic's market adjustment shows print days are relative-selling days for this
  stock (excess > 0 on 29 % of grade days vs 47 % ordinary), so no "coiled" credit is given.
- CCL (probability on the guide trigger from the company's own guidance history): applied, now on the
  complete seven-quarter record rather than the four the draft used; this is what moves KS4 up and
  KS1 down.
- MU (weight the primary metric; symmetric triggers): the UP switches (KS1, KS2, KS3) and the DOWN
  switch (KS4) sit on the same metric, the September level; KS5 is a flip condition only, and this
  time its probability is mixed into the sum.
- ACN (the company's own conventions; a crowded fear as upside): the convention record is the table
  above; the visible iPhone-18 fear (lukewarm Pro pre-orders, foldable slip, August consumer dip) is
  upside if the trillion headline prints, and that is inside scenarios A and B.
- One-way lean (research leaned DOWN 6 of 7 times, 5 wrong): this call is UP, set by the sum; it also
  matches the coin, so this card will not separate research from the coin on direction.
