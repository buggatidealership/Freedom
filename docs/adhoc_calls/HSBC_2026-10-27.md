## How I think the crowd digests it (my own read; H unless marked)
- HSBC's crowd has two clocks running: the quarter (easy comp, NII rising, Asia wealth) and the Budget tomorrow. Nobody who sold UK banks into a possible levy adds back the day before the announcement on a good quarter; the people who buy HSBC on this print are the ones who already hold it for Asia and the buyback. That makes the buyback line the swing: $1 B or more reads as "the capital story is intact despite the UK noise" and the stock recovers two to four points of the 15 it lost; $500 M, or a Hong Kong property charge in the ECL line, confirms the de-rating.
- The optics favour the print: +33 % profit against a comp that carried the BoCom hit, NII up for the first time in a while, and a stock that has already given back 15 %. The crowd's digestion favours caution: the Budget is 24 hours away, the UK bank index is at its most nervous since May, and HSBC's last two prints were an in-line sold (then −4.7 % on day two) and a miss sold.
- Net read: PBT ~$9.8-10.0 B, NII +3 % q/q, ECL slightly above the run-rate on Hong Kong CRE, buyback $500 M to $1 B, dividend 10c; London opens ±1 % and drifts −1 to −3 % into the Budget. P(up) 0.46.
