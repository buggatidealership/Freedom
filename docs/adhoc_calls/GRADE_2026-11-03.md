## New lessons (configuration, number, record, disproof)

1. **The forward line for a bookings-driven software name is bookings, and the trigger is deceleration, not sign.** Palantir beat and raised every guided line and was sold on TCV +6 % (from +49 %), US commercial TCV +11 % (from +153 %), RPO flat q/q and 64 large deals (from 73). KS2 asked whether US commercial TCV was *down* y/y; the crowd asked whether it had *decelerated by more than half*. Rule: on names at > 30× sales, carry a switch "bookings growth (TCV or RPO) below half the prior quarter's rate" at P ≥ 0.30, mapped to C, and let it outrank the revenue beat. Same error class as the Shopify v2 note on "the operational definition of decelerating" (which was right there and wrong here). Number next time: prior-quarter TCV growth / 2. Record: NOW (cRPO), RDDT (users), PLTR (TCV) — 3 of 3 growth names sold on a decelerating non-revenue line this fortnight. Disproof: two of the next five such prints close up ≥ +2 %.
2. **Defensive at its high after a run-in outranks the outside-tape table rule.** Pfizer: +21.5 % in three months, within 2 % of its 52-week high, beat EPS by 19 %, raised both FY lines, and closed down. The rule in force (Oct 27) said coin and small; I let the Oct 29 table rule override it because the table was raised. Hierarchy from tonight: inside the tape → regime; outside the tape → (a) dated overhang, (b) defensive at highs with d63 ≥ +15 % → no UP call, (c) table rule. Record for (b): PM, KO, NOVN-sized, PFE — raised or beaten tables on defensives at highs closed −1.2, −1.6, +1.0, −2.2: 1 of 4 up. Disproof: two of the next four such prints close up ≥ +2 %.
3. **The first scan sets the day's extreme, not the close, on this tape — now 3 of 3 today and 7 of the last 9 negative first scans.** AAPL −4.4 → −2; PLTR −8 → −1.65; SHOP −16 → −5.65 (AMZN −10 → −1.4, STX −6.9 → +4.5, GEV −6.6 → −1.9, GLW −4.7 → −1.1 earlier; INTC and RDDT extended). Rule for sizing a DOWN call on an after-close or pre-open print: the close lands at 0.3-0.5× the first scan unless the print is a C-scenario tail on a liquidation day. The Oct 29-30 "tails hold" hypothesis narrows to "tails hold on liquidation days"; on a bounce day (Nasdaq +0.8 %, chips rebounding) even a −16 % first scan was digested. Number next time: first-scan move × 0.4 as the size line, recorded beside the mixture and the conditional. Disproof: three of the next five negative first scans ≥ 4 % extend into the close.
4. **A scenario table can be right while the governing sentence is wrong, and the rubric must be able to say so.** SHOP's C leg named the revenue shortfall; the governing sentence named the Q4 guide. The rubric scored it "luck", which is too harsh for a table hit and too kind for a mechanism miss.
