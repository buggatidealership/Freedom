## How I think the crowd digests it (my own read; H unless marked)
- GE is the cleanest "beat, raise, sold" case on the record: three straight quarters of double-digit beats with guide raises, closed −7.4 %, −5.6 %, −4.1 % (M). The crowd has learned that the raise is the entry price, so the only print that gets bought is one that raises *more* than the street's own number — FY above ~$8.05 with CES growth lifted again and a 2028 framing update. Possible, but GE raised in April and July already; a third raise of that size would be the exception, not the pattern.
- The tape is the same one that sold ASML, TSMC, MS, IBKR: good news into 5 % yields gets sold, and the aerospace momentum complex has de-rated for a quarter (HWM −19 % over 63 days). The defense bid of last week (RTX, LMT) helps the group but not the "commercial aero cycle" story GE is priced on. Same-morning RTX could steal the narrative either way.
- The UP case is real and large: the stock is −18 % from its high with revisions flat, so a raise to ≥ $8.05 with LEAP deliveries and services growth intact squeezes +5 to +7 % (up median +6.3). I put it at 0.40, not 0.30, because the last three sales have reset expectations and GE's beats are reliably big.
- Net read: beat and raise to the low $8s, operating margin under last year's, the stock gaps down 2-4 % and the gap extends (4 of 4 gap-downs did). Modal close −4 %.
