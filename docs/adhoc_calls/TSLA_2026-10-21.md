- Tonight has two layers and the crowd knows which one matters. The numbers are pre-digested: flat revenue, a ~2.5 % operating margin, −$4.5 B of free cash flow, with the EPS bar cut 18 % to make a beat possible. That is zero new information for a stock that has already priced a jump in deal probability — the premium does not vanish, but the stock then trades on the first layer.
- The first layer in this tape is the capex and cash-flow switch (GE, Meta): $25 B of capex, negative FCF, and a 2027 framing that can only be "more". Tesla's own last five prints went 1 of 5 up on exactly this shape — weak numbers plus repeated promises — and Jul-26 was −14.5 % when the crowd stopped accepting "trust me" (S). The difference tonight is the bar has been cut and the merger gives the stock a floor of sorts; I expect a modest sale, not a crash.
- The reasons this could be wrong are large and specific. Musk reversed a −4.3 % gap into +2.3 % on the Oct-25 call; he can talk, and the merger is the kind of subject he does not resist. SpaceX's own print was bought +6.6 % today, so the Musk complex has a bid, and if NFLX is bought tonight the sold-print regime is over by my own rule. Options price only a 4.6 % move, which says the market treats tonight as the smaller event — that cuts the size, not the side.
