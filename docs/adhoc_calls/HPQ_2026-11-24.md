The person signing this outlook is an interim chief handing over in six weeks. Nobody in that seat sets a bar the next person might miss. The stock is 20 % off its high and 17 % lower in a month, so some of this is priced. But the number itself, a year of earnings under $3, would still be a headline, and the last two November outlooks were both sold.
