- What tips me DOWN is the skew, not the sign: the downside tail is three times the upside (−16 vs +10 in the history, Jan-26 showed the backlog number can gap the stock −9 % at the open), and a stock that closed on its high after +3.5 % needs more than "fine" to print +5 again. The crowd's sell-the-news reflex on a chased name has been the dominant pattern for eight sessions. The up case is real and the name's own history argues for it; I weight it just under even.
- Net read: backlog +25-27 % cc, cloud revenue ~+23 % cc, operating profit low-double-digit cc, guide reiterated, buyback on track; Xetra opens −1 to −3 % and closes near the gap. P(up) 0.47.
